01

The idea in one line

A calm, flat desk for a shop owner: one glance shows today's money, what needs attention, and that the AI is working safely on this laptop.

Who it is for

The owner of a single-branch pharmacy or shop, at a busy counter, often on an older laptop, sometimes reading in Hindi or Telugu. Every decision below is judged against that person, not against a design award.

Five principles

  1. Flat first. Solid shapes, clear edges, no gradients or glow on content. Depth is used only to separate layers: the photo, the frosted panels, and the content on them.
  2. Pastel marks importance. Butter and mint are not decoration. They mark the two things that matter most in each panel: today's figures and the main apps.
  3. Numbers are the heroes. Large, tabular figures (₹48,230.50, 1,076) with a one-line explanation under each.
  4. Trust is visible. The network state, the resource limits and what the AI is doing are always on screen, in the dock. They are controls, not decoration.
  5. Calm by default. One photo, softly blurred. Motion only where something is happening: the orb, the weather, a hover.

Style decisions already made

DecisionWhy
Flat design, not glass, neumorphism or skeuomorphismBest contrast and readability at a counter, cheapest to draw while a local AI model is using most of the machine, and matches Windows 11 and macOS.
Mica (frosted glass) on the panels and dock onlyLets the wallpaper show through without hurting text, because the content inside the panels stays solid.
Light theme onlyOne look to design, test and support. Dark mode was removed on purpose; see the improvements list for when to revisit.
A real photo, blurred 6 pxGives the screen warmth and place; the blur stops it competing with the content.
Everything bundled locallyThe app must work with the network switched off. No fonts, libraries or images load from the internet.

Type and layout

Typeface

Helvetica Neue, falling back to Helvetica, Arial Nova, then Arial. On Windows this usually renders as Arial, which is close enough at these sizes. One family throughout; hierarchy comes from size and weight.

Layout

  • Two panels side by side, widths 1 : 1.15, max 1280 px wide, 24 px gap and gutters.
  • App grid: 4 columns, 104 px rows, 10 px gaps, packed densely so no holes appear. Wide tiles span 2 columns; Findings spans 2 rows.
  • Dock: fixed, centred at the bottom, with the orb pill 10 px above it. Power button alone at top right.
  • Under 900 px wide the panels stack and the grid drops to 2 columns.

04

Components

Greeting card

  • Date as an uppercase eyebrow.
  • "Hello, Ravi". Click the name to switch user (owner Ravi, clerk Priya). In the app this comes from the login.
  • Weather art: a drawn sun with slowly turning rays and a cloud. Clear, Cloudy, Rain and Snow each change the art, and rain or snow also falls gently across the screen.
  • Line of the day: ten friendly prompts, one per day of the year.
  • Two figure cards, butter then mint: today's takings and bills today, each with a one-line explanation.
  • Ask box: a round input with a black Ask button. Asking starts the orb's searching → solving → composing run.

Apps panel

  • 13 tiles: icon chip, bold name, one-line status that says something live ("3 files waiting", "Audit at 18:00").
  • Shishya (butter, wide) is the front door. Make a Document (mint, wide) is the second.
  • Findings is tall because it carries two facts.
  • Badges: black for counts, peach for warnings.
  • Hover lifts a tile 2 px; press shrinks it slightly.

Motion

  • Orb: always moving gently; speed comes from the library's tuned presets.
  • Sun rays turn once every 40 seconds. Rain and snow are drawn on one canvas.
  • Popovers fade and rise 8 px over 0.2 s. Tiles lift on hover.
  • With "reduce motion" switched on in Windows, all of this stops and the orb shows a still frame.

Wallpaper

An alpine valley photo at dawn, saved as web/wallpaper.jpg (2000 × 1125, 159 KB), blurred 6 px and scaled 4% so the blur's soft edge never shows.

How it is built

docs/design/This guide's source and its images.

How to change things safely

  • A colour: edit the token in the :root block. Do not add new colours inside components.
  • A tile: copy an existing <button class="tile">. Add wide or tall to change its size. Keep the total so the grid fills without holes.
  • The wallpaper: replace wallpaper.jpg with any landscape photo about 2000 px wide and under 300 KB. Darker photos need a stronger Mica (raise the 70% in --mica).
  • The orb's states: the labels live in the LABEL list in the last script block.
  • The dial ranges: in the DIALS list (CPU 10–100 by 10, RAM 2–16 by 1).
  • The daily lines: in the LINES list (ten lines).
Still demo-only Takings, bills, tile statuses, weather, CPU and RAM values and the network switch are sample data. Clicking a tile only shows a message. Wiring each one to the real app is the next engineering step.

What to improve next

Ranked by how much each matters to a shop owner at the counter. High before the December submission, Medium if there is time, Later after.

HighHindi and Telugu text may show as boxesHelvetica and Arial have no Devanagari or Telugu letters. Bundle Noto Sans Devanagari and Noto Sans Telugu in web/fonts/ and add them to the font stack.
HighWeather needs the internet, but the sandbox is offlineFetch it on the host side only (outside the sandbox), cache it, and fall back to a time-of-day sun or moon when offline. Never let the sandbox fetch it.

Checklist before every change ships

  • Readable from arm's length, on the darkest part of the wallpaper.
  • Uses existing tokens only; no new colours.
  • Works with the network off.
  • Works with "reduce motion" on.
  • Checked at 1280 × 800 and 1920 × 1080, and on the Mac.
  • Every control reachable with the keyboard, with a visible focus ring.
